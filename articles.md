# Briefing 2: themes and articles

Seven themes, one group on each. Your group picks a theme in class and reads
the articles listed under it. Every article here was published between
**June 29 and September 18, 2026**.

The news stories came through the AAAI AI-Alert digest. The research articles
are from *AI Magazine*, AAAI's journal, and every one of them is free to read.

---
